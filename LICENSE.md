## 2. Eligibility

Subject to the platform permissions in section 1 and the contribution exception in section 4, this license permits ordinary use at no charge by individuals acting on their own behalf and organizations that meet all three requirements:

- ten or fewer developers in total
- less than $1,000,000 (USD) in gross revenue for the measurement period defined below
- less than $1,000,000 (USD) in cumulative funds raised to date

The developer count includes current employees and contractors who perform software development for the organization, whether or not they use Locali. Entities under common control are counted together, and their respective revenue and funds raised are aggregated, excluding transfers within that group to avoid double counting. For example, an organization with eleven developers does not qualify even if only one of them uses Locali.

Gross revenue is measured over the most recently completed fiscal year. Until an organization completes its first fiscal year, use gross revenue recognized from formation through the assessment date. Revenue eligibility is reassessed at each fiscal year end, or continuously during the first fiscal year. Developer count and cumulative funding are assessed as they change.

Funds raised means the cumulative financing received through equity investments, capital contributions, debt financing, and funding grants. It excludes ordinary customer payments for goods or services already accounted for as revenue. Spending funds or repaying financing does not reduce the cumulative amount raised. The funding requirement remains applicable after the organization begins recognizing revenue; revenue and funding are separate limits, not alternative tests.

An organization meets the developer requirement at exactly ten developers. It does not meet a financial requirement at exactly $1,000,000 under the applicable measure.
